# Ranking Methodology

Gatilab Blog Rankings combines reproducible public signals with documented human editorial review. Public data helps create and monitor the candidate pool. Editorial judgment determines the final order.

## Tracks

Every category has two separate rankings:

- **Independent Blogs:** individual creators and small editorial teams whose publication is the primary product or identity.
- **Publisher and Company Blogs:** publications owned by companies, institutions, media groups, product businesses, or larger editorial organizations.

Track assignment is reviewed independently from rank.

## Eligibility

A publication must:

- publish primarily in English for the current edition;
- make most editorial content publicly readable;
- have a stable canonical domain and identifiable editorial owner;
- publish original editorial work;
- show substantive activity within the previous 180 days, unless its category has a documented slower cadence;
- disclose material sponsorship, affiliate, and ownership relationships where relevant;
- fit at least one published category definition.

Scrapers, press-release feeds, coupon sites, pure product changelogs, abandoned sites, malware risks, and undisclosed mass-rewritten publications are excluded.

## Scoring

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Editorial quality and originality | 30% |
| Expertise, trust, and transparency | 20% |
| Reach and authority | 20% |
| Consistency and freshness | 15% |
| UX, accessibility, and technical quality | 10% |
| Community or cultural impact | 5% |

Reviewers record whole-number component scores from 0 to 100. The generator applies the weights and calculates the final score to 1 decimal place.

Reach signals are interpreted within a category and track. Third-party audience or traffic values are estimates, not verified readership.

## Review Confidence

- **High:** 2 reviewers and current evidence across all components.
- **Standard:** 1 reviewer with current automated and editorial evidence.
- **Provisional:** new, disputed, incomplete, or awaiting a second review.

Provisional entries may appear in beta rankings but cannot receive a Category Winner badge.

## Ties

Exact-score ties are resolved by:

1. editorial quality and originality;
2. expertise, trust, and transparency;
3. most recent full review;
4. stable alphabetical order when the evidence still does not separate the entries.

## Update Cycle

- Automated eligibility and change checks run monthly.
- New, changed, disputed, provisional, and Top 25 entries receive monthly editorial attention.
- Every ranked entry enters a complete editorial reassessment on a quarterly rotation.
- Every published edition is tagged and preserved.

## Corrections and Appeals

Anyone may open a public correction or appeal. Factual corrections can be accepted immediately. Rank changes normally enter the next edition unless fraud, plagiarism, malware, or another safety issue requires suspension.

## Commercial Separation

Ranking positions cannot be bought. Review timing, category placement, track placement, scoring, badges, and winner status cannot be exchanged for money, services, links, products, or access.

## Limits

A rank is an editorial comparison inside one category, track, edition, and evidence window. It is not certification of every claim, product, author, or business associated with a publication.
