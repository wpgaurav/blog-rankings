# Code of Conduct

Participate in good faith. Be specific, evidence-led, and respectful of publishers, creators, reviewers, and contributors.

Harassment, threats, impersonation, doxxing, fabricated evidence, coordinated manipulation, and undisclosed paid influence are not accepted.

Public issues should contain only information needed to evaluate the nomination, correction, or appeal. Do not post private analytics, credentials, personal addresses, or non-public correspondence.
